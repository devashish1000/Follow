MASCOT SPEC — “NOA” THE OWL + MINI-BRIEFCASE (NEXTINTERVIEW)
Lock this so Stitch/Rork can reuse one asset mental model:
Character: A smart, prepared owl—adult-professional, calm confidence. Study/career tone via geometry + briefcase, not cartoons, graduation caps, or baby proportions.
Construction language: Simple circles + triangles only (plus rounded rects for briefcase). No feather texture, no gradients on the mascot, no glossy eyes—flat fills + strokes like SF-style icons.
Silhouette (bold geometric):
Head/body: One large circle or slightly flattened circle for the head; body is a smaller circle or rounded trapezoid tucked under (or a single merged “pear” of two circles)—pick one fusion style and keep it on every frame.
Ear tufts: Two small upward triangles on top of the head OR smooth head with no tufts—choose tufts ON everywhere (recommended for “owl” read at small size). Same height, same outward angle every time.
Eyes: Two identical circles (slightly large for warmth but not “googly”); tiny solid pupils centered or slightly inward for focus—same pupil rule always. Optional thin curved “spectacle” arc as a single 2px stroke around both eyes—if used, always the same arc; if not used, never add later.
Beak: One downward-pointing triangle or soft diamond, centered; same size and angle on every screen.
Wings: Minimal—two rounded triangles or half-circles flush to the body, no finger feathers.
Feet (optional): Tiny three-line perch or omit for clarity at small sizes—lock per theme (either always on or always off).
Mini-briefcase (signature prop): Always present when the mascot appears. Compact horizontal rounded rectangle with one small handle arc on top; single vertical line or slight latch notch for detail—no buckles, stickers, or logos. Held at the side (one wing implied as support) or in front at chest height—same default grip on A/B; other screens may rotate ≤15° but same case proportions. Fill: ink text.primary in light mode; ivory or surface.elevated-adjacent in dark mode—same geometry every time.
Line / fill rules (match SF icon weight): 2.0px stroke on outer contours; flat fills for eyes (white/ivory), pupils (ink or charcoal), beak (optional gold #C7A35A only for beak—if chosen, always); briefcase as solid fill or stroke + subtle fill—one system per theme, consistent everywhere.
Color (plumage): Light mode: warm charcoal #3D3835 strokes with soft cream #F0E8DC belly circle or stroke-only—pick one and keep consistent. Dark mode: ivory/cream #F6F2EA on charcoal #0E1117 surfaces—same silhouette. Optional tiny teal #0F766E accent only as spectacle stroke or one wing highlight—if used, same placement/size rules on every frame.
Size on screen: Onboarding A/B: owl + bubble ≤ ~18% viewport height total. Empty/error states: may be slightly larger but same construction—never a detailed illustration pass.
Name for annotations (optional): “Noa”—designer notes only; UI copy need not use the name.
